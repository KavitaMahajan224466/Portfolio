The Person Behind the Work

I grew up in India, where I was introduced to computer science in middle school. It was one of those subjects that just clicked for me. That curiosity eventually led me to study Computer Science and later move to the United States, where I’ve had the opportunity to build a career doing what I genuinely enjoy.

My career began in software engineering and gradually expanded into technical leadership, enterprise architecture and strategic advisory. Looking back, one of the most defining transitions was moving from building systems to helping organizations make technology decisions. It changed how I think about technology—not just how to build it, but how it creates value for businesses and the people they serve.

Today, my work centers on enterprise AI. What excites me most isn’t simply the rapid pace of innovation, but the opportunity to make powerful technologies more accessible. I enjoy helping organizations understand what’s possible and helping builders gain the confidence to experiment, learn and create with technologies that were once available only to specialists.

Outside work, I enjoy chasing ideas that spark my curiosity, whether through thoughtful conversations, articles, talks, travel, or simply observing the world around me. My family keeps me grounded and reminds me to see familiar things with fresh eyes.
