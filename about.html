The Person Behind the Work

I was introduced to computer science in middle school, and it was one of those subjects that just clicked for me. That curiosity eventually led me to study Computer Science and later move to the United States, where I’ve had the opportunity to build a career doing what I genuinely enjoy.

My career began in software engineering and expanded into technical leadership, enterprise architecture, and strategic advisory. The most defining shift was moving from building systems to helping organizations make technology decisions—it taught me to look beyond the technology itself, to understand the problem, the business context, and how technology can create something genuinely useful.

Today, my focus is enterprise AI. What excites me isn’t just the pace of innovation, but making powerful technology more accessible—helping organizations see what’s possible, and helping builders gain the confidence to experiment, learn, and create with tools that were once available only to specialists.

Outside of work, I’m drawn to good conversations, new ideas, travel, and anything that gives me a different way of seeing the world. My family keeps me grounded and reminds me to see familiar things with fresh eyes.
